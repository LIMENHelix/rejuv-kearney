The menu

What we actually do.

No miracle claims. No cookie-cutter protocols. We measure first, treat what is actually there, and tell you the truth about what will—and won't—help.

Not sure where to start? Start with labs

Testosterone & hormone health

Comprehensive hormone panels, symptom review, and provider-managed TRT with regular monitoring when it is clinically appropriate. We watch the numbers that matter—including hematocrit and PSA.

Medical weight loss

GLP-1 care built around your labs and your life. We use branded tirzepatide (Zepbound® / Mounjaro®) only—never compounded—prescribed when appropriate, with nutrition and training support.

ED & sexual health

A private conversation and real options, including sildenafil and tadalafil when prescribed. No awkward waiting room. No judgment. Just a provider who has had this conversation before.

Full-body blood work

CBC, CMP, lipids, A1c, thyroid, PSA, vitamin D, testosterone, and more. A clear read on what is going on—and a provider to explain what to do next. Lab panels from $100.

Muscle, strength & performance

Body composition, strength markers, training guidance, and recovery protocols that fit a grown man's schedule. Build muscle without pretending you're still 22.

Longevity & healthspan

Quarterly deep panels including ApoB, A1c, hsCRP, and hormones. Body-comp and grip-strength tracking, sleep and nutrition coaching, and a provider-reviewed plan built on data.

PRP & joint care

Provider-administered PRP for knees, shoulders, and hips—a non-surgical option worth discussing with your provider. Cash add-on, no operating room required.

Advanced protocols

Peptides are not part of our launch menu. We are waiting on FDA listings and legal approval before we offer them. Join the interest list and we will tell you when that changes.

The honest answers

Questions men actually ask.

Do you take insurance?
We are a cash-pay clinic and do not bill insurance. HSA and FSA cards are accepted, and we provide documentation you can submit to your insurer. Ask us about financing options before you commit.
What does the first visit cost?
The 15-minute consult is complimentary. Lab panels start at $100. If you and your provider decide on treatment, you will get the plan and the price in writing before you commit. No surprise invoices.
Are prescriptions guaranteed?
No. Every prescription requires an evaluation by a licensed provider. Not everyone qualifies, and we will tell you if you don't. That is the point of doing labs and a real provider visit instead of an online questionnaire.
Is this confidential?
Yes. Private rooms, discreet communication, and no waiting-room gossip. We do not sell your information. Please do not put medical details in the website form—we will collect those securely during intake.
Do you offer telehealth?
Coaching and follow-ups can be virtual. Labs and provider visits happen in Kearney, and some treatments require in-person care. Your first consult can be on Zoom or in person—your call.
What is the story with the bourbon?
The first 100 men who book a consult are Founding 100 members. After your visit, you can pick up a complimentary fifth of bourbon. 21+ only, while supplies last, no purchase necessary. ID required at pickup. Pickup is offsite—alcohol is not served at the clinic. Please drink responsibly.
Do you offer peptides?
Not at launch. We are waiting on FDA listings and legal approval before we add peptides to the menu. Join the interest list and we will contact you when that changes—we would rather be honest than sell you something we cannot stand behind.
What ages do you treat?
Adult men. If you are under 18, this clinic is not the right fit. If you are in a medical emergency, call 911. If you are in mental-health crisis, call or text 988.
